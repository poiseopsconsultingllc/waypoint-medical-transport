The Story

Why Waypoint exists

A waypoint is a fixed point on a route. It is how you know you are on course and how you know you will make it where you are going. That is the job here: being the reliable point between a veteran's front door and the appointment that matters.

John Noffsinger is a veteran and the owner of Noffsinger Services LLC in Canton, Ohio. Waypoint Medical Transport is his answer to a problem he kept seeing: veterans missing VA appointments, or dreading them, because getting there was the hardest part of the day.

Missing a VA appointment is not a small thing. It can mean months of waiting for the next slot. So Waypoint treats every pickup like it is the one that cannot be missed, because it is.

The same care goes into the delivery side. When a prescription, a lab run or a piece of medical equipment has to be somewhere, it rides with someone who treats it like it matters. Because it does.

No call centers. No apps. When you call Waypoint, you are talking to the owner. That is how a Canton business should work.

On Time
