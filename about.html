About John

Meet the founder

John Noffsinger spent five years as a U.S. Navy hospital corpsman, the medical backbone of the Navy and Marine Corps. He cared for patients on their hardest days and responded when seconds mattered.

He went on to earn a bachelor's degree in Healthcare Administration and is completing his Master of Healthcare Administration. He understands both sides of every appointment: the person in the seat and the healthcare system they are navigating.

Waypoint is that training put to work. Patient care that starts in the driveway, not the waiting room.

No call centers. No apps required. When you call Waypoint, you are talking to the person responsible for getting you there. That is how a Louisville business should work.
