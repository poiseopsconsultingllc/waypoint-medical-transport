Services & Pricing

Where We Take You

Reliable rides for the appointments that keep your care on track, with pricing you see before you ride.

Rides

We provide reliable rides to and from:

  • Dialysis treatments, standing weekly schedules welcome
  • Doctor and specialist appointments
  • Hospital discharges, going home safely, same day
  • Outpatient procedures and surgeries
  • Physical therapy and rehabilitation
  • Lab work, imaging, and testing
  • Pharmacy stops on the way home
Service Levels

The right ride for every rider.

Wheelchair-Accessible Transportation

A real wheelchair van, not a retrofitted sedan. Dual wheelchair positions, professional securement and tie-downs, smooth entry ramp. Available from day one.

Ambulatory Rides

For riders who walk or use a walker. Same comfortable van, same door-through-door help, every trip.

Round Trips & Standing Orders

Round trips, one-way rides, and recurring standing orders. Set up a weekly schedule once and never think about it again.

Caregivers Ride Free

Family members and caregivers ride along free. Always. No fine print.

How It Works

Three steps. That's it.

No apps to download, no accounts to create. Booking a ride should be the easiest part of your day.

01

Book your ride

Call (330) 942-7306 or use the booking form. Book as far ahead as you like; next-day rides need 24 hours' notice when possible.

02

We confirm everything

Pickup time, address, destination, and fare, so there are no surprises.

03

Ride with a professional

Your driver arrives early, helps you from your door to the vehicle, and walks you inside to your appointment. When you're done, we're there for the trip home.

Hours Monday-Friday from 5:00 AM (early dialysis riders, we've got you) Saturdays 5:00 AM to 12 PM Sunday closed Need a ride outside these hours? Ask about our after-hours service. (330) 942-7306
