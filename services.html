Services

What Waypoint Does

Every job comes down to the same promise: on time, with care. Here is what that covers.

VA Appointment Rides

Door-to-door rides for veterans to VA appointments anywhere in Ohio: Cleveland, Columbus, Cincinnati, Dayton, Chillicothe and every clinic between. We track the appointment time, not just the pickup time.

Our specialty

Wheelchair-Friendly Transport

Need help getting in and out, or room for a wheelchair or walker? Say so when you book and we will set the trip up right. Ask about what fits your situation.

Just ask when booking

Medical Deliveries & Courier

Prescriptions, lab specimens, medical supplies, equipment and documents. Picked up on schedule, handled with care, and confirmed when they land.

Private Pay Trips

Booking for yourself or a family member? Any appointment, VA or not. Simple per-trip pricing quoted up front. No memberships, no subscriptions, no surprises.

Recurring Rides

Standing appointments like dialysis, therapy or weekly check-ins go on the schedule once and stay there. Same driver whenever we can make it happen.

Government & Contract Work

Transport and courier work for agencies, counties and facilities. Noffsinger Services LLC is set up on SAM.gov for government contracting. Contract officers, let's talk.
