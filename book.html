FAQ

Questions families ask.

Can a family member or caregiver ride along?

Yes. Always, and always free.

Do you help with walkers, canes, and mobility?

Absolutely. Door-through-door assistance is included with every ride, and our driver is trained in patient care and transfers.

How far ahead should I book?

Book as far ahead as you like. For next-day rides, 24 hours' notice is ideal. Standing weekly schedules (like dialysis) can be set up once and never thought about again.

Do you take insurance or Medicaid?

We accept private pay and all major cards today.

How do I pay?

By card when you book. You or a family member can pay from anywhere using our Pay My Fare page. Cash accepted in person. Want to know the number first? Get an instant estimate.

What if my appointment runs long?

We build flexibility into every pickup. For short visits we can wait on-site; for longer treatments we return when you call. No stress, no rush.

Do you do long trips?

Yes. Cleveland, Akron, Columbus specialist visits and more, by arrangement.

What if I need to cancel?

More than 24 hours ahead: free. Same-day cancellation: $25. If we arrive and the ride is a no-show, the base fare applies. Life happens, so every customer gets one "oops" waived per year.
