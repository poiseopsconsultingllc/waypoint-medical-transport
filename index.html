The Promise

Every ride, handled with care.

Getting to dialysis, doctor visits, and therapy shouldn't be the hardest part of your care. Waypoint Medical Transport is built on a simple standard: safe, comfortable, and on time. Every trip, without exception.

Your driver meets you at your door, assists you to the vehicle, and stays with you until you're checked in at your appointment. When you're finished, we're there for the ride home.

That's door-through-door service. That's the Waypoint standard.
